Varense / Brand system / v1.0

One hue, two signals, and a mark that draws what the engine computes.

The identity is a single espresso luminance ramp. Colour is not decoration here — it is reserved, and it means something specific twice.

01 — The mark

The envelope, and the path inside it.

A domed envelope spanning the full range of outcomes, a baseline, and the realised path climbing through the middle of it. The envelope is where the portfolio could have gone. The path is where it went.

The baseline is load-bearing, not ornament. Without it the dome collapses into the signal-strength glyph every device in the world already uses; with it, the shape can only be read as a chart.

One ink, one stroke weight, no gradient and no fill above 24px — so it reproduces identically in foil, embroidery, and a 16-pixel favicon.

03 — Icon & favicon

Every size step removes detail from the path and never from the envelope. The dome and its baseline are the identity; the path is the story they contain, and it is the only part allowed to simplify.
